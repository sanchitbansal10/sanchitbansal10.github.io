## Work

The last time I wrote one of these, I was still in an internal accelerator-style program at work. That’s long done — a lot has happened since.

**Cross-team initiative.** A cross-team effort to change identity verification requirements — I owned our team’s slice end to end, largely on my own and without much manager involvement. Several services we maintain, plus some help shaping the overall architecture. Shipped, tested, and rolled out to full traffic without issues. Another group owned the wider program; our scope was our components, but delivery on our side was mine.

**Parsing experiment (in progress).** An onboarding flow depends on extracting structure from uploaded documents; we’re testing a swap from a legacy parser to a newer ML-backed one. I’m leading the effort — most of the build is done, with a few pieces still in flight with teammates. Hoping to launch soon.

**Recommendations & batch inference (just starting).** I’ve been asked to drive a new backend-heavy project with ML infra and the modeling team — still in the investigation phase. At a high level it’s about nudging users to refresh profile data and serving recommendations from models that already exist. Less room to “fix it in the UI later”; mistakes are costlier here. It’s likely the biggest scope I’ve taken on so far. Wish me luck.
